## Requirements

- PHP 8.2+
- Laravel 10, 11, or 12

> **Note:** Laravel 10 and 11 are end-of-life and carry known, unpatched
> upstream security advisories. Probe itself remains compatible with those
> versions, but running on an EOL Laravel release means your application
> inherits those open advisories regardless of anything Probe does. Upgrading
> to Laravel 12 is recommended where possible.
